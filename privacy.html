This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It loads no fonts or scripts from other servers.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and the time of the request. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choice of language and device view is kept in your browser only. It is never sent to us.

The app

  • No account, no server of ours. cardXchange keeps your data on your devices and in your private iCloud. Apple stores it; we have no access to it.
  • Apple Contacts. cardXchange reads your address book to show your people. It writes only what you ask for: a link to the person, relationships, accepted card updates and, on the Mac if you switch it on, a short history in the note. The text of your notes never leaves the device.
  • Screenshots, business cards and photos are read on the device, with Apple's on-device frameworks.
  • Map. To show addresses on the map, the street, postal code, city and country of each address are sent to Apple's geocoding service. Your own position is shown on the map, but it is not stored and not sent.
  • Card exchange (coming). The card that you choose is shared, through iCloud, with the people you exchange cards with. You can end each link.
