Privacy

Your data stays with you.

This website

This website sets no cookies. To count visits, it uses Google Analytics 4 by Google Ireland Limited, without cookies: the consent to store analytics data stays denied, so Google stores nothing on your device. For each page view, your browser sends Google the page, the referring page, the time, the language, the screen size and your IP address. Google uses the IP address to find the country and does not store it. Google can process this data in the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). A content blocker in your browser stops these signals. Details are in Google's privacy policy.

This is the one script from another server. The website loads no fonts from other servers.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and the time of the request. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choice of language and device view is kept in your browser only. It is never sent to us.

The app

  • No account, no server of ours. cardXchange keeps your data on your devices and in your private iCloud. Apple stores it; we have no access to it.
  • Apple Contacts. cardXchange reads your address book to show your people. It writes only what you ask for: a link to the person, relationships, accepted card updates and, on the Mac if you switch it on, a short history in the note. The text of your notes never leaves the device.
  • Screenshots, business cards and photos are read on the device, with Apple's on-device frameworks.
  • Map. To show addresses on the map, the street, postal code, city and country of each address are sent to Apple's geocoding service. Your own position is shown on the map, but it is not stored and not sent.
  • Card exchange. When you show a card code, the fields that you chose for your card are shared through iCloud with the one person who scans the code. That person also gets each later change of your card. Until the person accepts it, or until the code ends after 24 hours, a copy of the card is in the public part of cardXchange's iCloud storage, not in your private iCloud. Anyone who has the code can read this copy during that time. Your app deletes it after the accept or after the code ended, the next time it opens.
